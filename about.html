We started CyberMagnet with a simple belief: every business deserves access to premium digital growth systems — not just the ones with enterprise budgets.

Founder Photo — Benjamin McCarrell III
The Founder

Benjamin McCarrell III

Benjamin didn't start CyberMagnet in a boardroom. He started it in the community — watching talented business owners struggle with outdated websites, invisible search presence, and marketing that didn't deliver.

With a deep background in SEO, digital strategy, and automation, Benjamin built CyberMagnet to bridge the gap between what large corporations access and what local businesses can afford. His vision: give every business owner the same tools, strategies, and systems that the biggest brands use — but tailored, personal, and built for real results.

Today, CyberMagnet is a growing team of strategists, designers, and engineers dedicated to helping businesses attract more customers, build stronger brands, and operate with the efficiency of companies ten times their size.
